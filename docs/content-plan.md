## Content source

The [current Farm Natura homepage](https://www.farmnatura.in/) describes a managed natural-farming estate in Kandukur by Planet Green Infra. It reports 110+ acres, six-plus years of soil revitalisation, four years of managed maintenance, indigenous seeds, and individual ownership. These are statements from the current site, not independently verified facts.

Keep location, farming, ownership, and family life central to the rewrite. Confirm plot sizes, availability, prices, maintenance terms, travel times, legal claims, and farmhouse permissions with the business before final copy. Do not invent certifications, owner reviews, events, or delivery services.

The homepage lists the contact number **+91 95795 55666** and an office above Hyundai Showroom on Q-City Road, Financial District, Gowlidoddi, Hyderabad. Distinguish the office from the estate location.
